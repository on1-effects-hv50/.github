## Photo Styling Overview
ON1 Effects treats every look as a stack of filters you can reorder, blend, and mask independently. This modular approach lets you build a signature style once and reuse it across an entire body of work.

The library includes film emulations, glows, borders, textures, and color grades, each tunable with sliders. Because filters are non-destructive, you can dial any element back long after the initial edit.

Precise masking sits at the core, letting you paint effects exactly where they belong. Luminosity and color range masks target skies, shadows, or skin without tedious manual selection.

## Getting Started Playbook
Open an image and add your first filter, such as a base color grade, then stack a film look on top. Adjust each filter's opacity and blend mode until the balance feels right for your subject.

When the stack looks good, paint a mask to keep sharpening off soft backgrounds or hold color in a sky. Save the finished combination as a preset so future edits start from your established style.

## Everyday Use
In routine work, users apply a saved preset to a batch, then tweak masks per image for context-aware polish. The stack stays live, so switching a film look or lowering a texture takes seconds and never degrades the original file.
